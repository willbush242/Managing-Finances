This spreadsheet is intended for use by an individual in a house with multiple people where that individual manages the bills of the house with the other occupants paying the individual a regular monthly contribution to the bills. All occupants are contributing an equal amount.

Select sheet appropriate for your situation. Simply fill in the "Money Out" and "Money In" boxes and let the spreadsheet show you households spending on bills each month. It will show you who owes you money for the bills, whether you have extra money left over to spend on bills in future months or whether you need to be compensated by other members of your household.

Once one month has passed, if everyone has paid their share, you should not need any boxes from previous months - everything will update. So, if in September there is £50 extra left over after everyone has paid their set amount and then in October there is £25 extra the box in Column H/Net Money for October will be green and show that you now have £75 extra.

The pink box is a quick way of identifying who has not paid their share for a month as their number will be less than everyone else.

Importantly, always make sure the "Money In" box is updated. If Net Money is very high and you had to top it up yourself to settle the bills then others owe you money. If you get them to compensate you, make sure to add that on top of what they have already paid that month in the "Money In" box.
